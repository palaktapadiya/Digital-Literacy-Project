Social Media Do's

Keep profiles professional — Use a clear profile photo, write a proper bio, and keep your information accurate and up to date on all platforms.
Share your achievements — Post about certifications, projects, hackathons, and academic milestones to build a positive digital presence that impresses future employers.
Engage respectfully — When commenting or replying online, always use polite and constructive language even when you disagree with someone's opinion.
Use privacy settings — Regularly review and update your privacy settings on Instagram, Facebook, and other platforms to control who can see your personal content.
Give credit — When sharing someone else's work, article, or image online, always tag or credit the original creator to respect intellectual property.


Social Media Don'ts

Don't overshare personal information — Never publicly post your home address, phone number, college ID, bank details, or daily routine as it makes you vulnerable to cybercrime.
Don't post offensive content — Avoid posting anything that is rude, discriminatory, or disrespectful toward any individual, community, religion, or gender — it can have serious legal consequences.
Don't spread misinformation — Always verify news and information before sharing it. Sharing fake news, edited images, or unverified claims can mislead others and damage your credibility.
Don't vent about college or professors — Publicly complaining about faculty, exams, or institutions on social media can lead to disciplinary action and permanently damage your academic reputation.
Don't ignore suspicious activity — If you receive unusual messages, friend requests from strangers, or suspicious links — do not respond or click. Report and block immediately.
